> [!info]
> Feed the starter Friday night. It should double in 6 to 8 hours.

The dough sits at 72% hydration:

$$
\text{hydration} = \frac{360\,\text{g water}}{500\,\text{g flour}} = 72\%
$$

| Ingredient | Grams | Baker's % |
|---|---|---|
| Bread flour | 450 | 90% |
| Whole wheat | 50 | 10% |
| Water | 360 | 72% |
| Starter | 100 | 20% |
| Salt | 10 | 2% |

- [x] Autolyse 45 min
- [x] Four sets of stretch and folds
- [ ] Shape and cold proof overnight
- [ ] Bake 250 °C lid on, 20 min, then 230 °C

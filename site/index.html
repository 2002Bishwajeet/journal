Private notes, encrypted.

Journal is an encrypted Markdown notebook. Your notes stay home: on your device, and encrypted on a Homebase identity you own.

Open Journal Join the Homebase wait list

Homebase gives you a private web address that holds your data. Journal signs in with it. Homebase sign-up is a closed beta with a wait list.

Where your notes live

On your device first, encrypted on your Homebase, and shared only when you choose.

  1. On your device

    Every note is kept in a database on your device, so Journal opens fast and works offline. The Journal apps encrypt this copy too.

  2. Encrypted on your Homebase

    Notes sync encrypted to your Homebase identity, a private space that belongs to you, and on to your other devices.

  3. Shared by choice

    Share a note with people you know, or publish it as a public page. A public page is not encrypted: anyone with the link can read it.

Questions

Something missing? Ask on GitHub.

Are my notes encrypted?

Yes, on your Homebase, in every version of Journal. The Journal apps for iPhone, Android and desktop also encrypt the copy on your device. Public pages are not encrypted, because anyone with the link can read them.

Do I need an account?

Journal signs in with a Homebase identity, a private space that belongs to you. To get one, join the Homebase wait list.

Does Journal work offline?

Yes. Your notes live in a database on your device, so Journal opens fast and keeps working offline, then syncs when you're back.

Is there an app for my phone or desktop?

Yes. Journal for iPhone and iPad is in the App Store, and Journal for Mac, Windows and Linux is a download. Android is coming later; until then, install Journal from the browser to your phone's home screen. Download Journal, or read about the apps.

Can I move from Notion or Obsidian?

Yes. Import a Notion Markdown & CSV export or an Obsidian vault as a .zip in Settings → Data & storage. Links, images, tags and callouts come with it.

Is Journal open source?

Yes, under the AGPL-3.0. The code is on GitHub.

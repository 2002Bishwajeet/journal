Where your notes live

On your device first. Sync is a layer on top, not the place your notes live.

  1. Write on your device

    Every note is stored in a database in your browser. Journal opens fast and works with no connection.

  2. Sync it, encrypted

    Notes sync encrypted across your devices through your Homebase identity, a space that belongs to you.

  3. Share only what you choose

    Share a note with people you know, or publish it as a public page. Everything else stays private.

Agents get in only where you grant a folder or a note, everything exports as markdown in a ZIP, and the code is open source under the AGPL-3.0.

The copy on your device is not encrypted yet; passkey or passphrase unlock is in progress.

Everyday writing, done well

Tasks, callouts and tables

Markdown shortcuts as you type, and a slash menu for the rest: tables, task lists, callouts, toggles and footnotes.

Math and code

LaTeX equations, inline or as their own block, next to syntax-highlighted code.

Every earlier version

Restore an earlier version of a note. It comes back as one edit, so a single undo puts things as they were.

On your phone too

Install Journal as an app on your phone, tablet or computer, in light or dark mode.

Also in the editor

  • Folders and tags. Filter by tag, or delete a tag from every note at once.
  • Daily notes. One click opens today's note, from your own template.
  • Search. Full text across all notes, with recent searches.
  • Note links. Type [[ to link another note, or create it on the spot.
  • Find and replace. Inside a note, with match case and whole words.
  • Galleries. Drop several images for a row, grid, masonry or carousel.
  • Templates. Start new notes from templates you write.
  • Import. Bring in a Notion export or an Obsidian vault.
  • Writing help. An optional assistant on desktop that runs in your browser.

Share it, or publish it

Share a note with people you know, who can edit it with you. Or publish it from your own space as a clean public page.

With people you know

Share with another Homebase identity or a whole circle. They find the note under ‘Shared with me’, edits merge, and you can revoke access at any time.

As a public page

A public note gets an ‘On this page’ outline, its cover image, highlighted code and working live blocks. Public notes are not encrypted, by design.

Blocks that run in the note

Write Mermaid and get a diagram. Write SVG and get a drawing. Write HTML or React and get a working page: a calculator, a chart, a small dashboard.

It runs inside your note, and on your public page too. React blocks can use Recharts, Lucide icons, d3, three, lodash, mathjs and papaparse, plus a kit of components in Journal's style.

Questions

What people ask before they move their notes. Something missing? Ask on GitHub.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Journal signs in with a Homebase identity, your own private space. Homebase is invite-only during its alpha, so join its wait list.

Are my notes encrypted?

The synced copy is encrypted. The copy on your device is not encrypted yet; passkey or passphrase unlock is in progress. Public notes are not encrypted, by design.

Can I move from Notion or Obsidian?

Yes. Import a Notion Markdown & CSV export or an Obsidian vault as a .zip in Settings → Data & storage. Links, images, tags and callouts come with it.

Does AI see my notes?

Agents connected through MCP see only the folders and notes you grant. The optional writing assistant runs on your device.

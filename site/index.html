Your notes on your device. Private by design.

Journal keeps every note in a database inside your browser, so it opens fast and works offline. It syncs them encrypted to your own private space, and lets AI agents build live, interactive pages inside them, only where you allow.

Where your notes live

Your notes live on your device first. Sync is a layer on top, not the place your notes live.

  1. Your device

    Write on your device.

    Every note is stored in a database in your browser. Journal opens fast and works with no connection.

  2. Your private space

    Sync to your own private space.

    Sign in with your Homebase identity (homebase.id). Your notes sync encrypted across your devices, on an identity that belongs to you.

  3. People / public page

    Share only what you choose.

    Share a note with people you know, or publish it as a public page. Everything else stays private.

Journal needs a Homebase identity today. The copy on your device is not yet encrypted; passkey or passphrase unlock is in progress.

A notebook that does the everyday well

  • Markdown editor

  • Math and code

    LaTeX equations and syntax-highlighted code.

  • Folders and tags

Install it

Install Journal as an app on your phone, tablet or computer. Light and dark mode.

Share with people. Publish to everyone.

With people you know

Share a note with another Homebase identity or a whole circle. They find it under ‘Shared with me’ and can edit it. Edits merge, and you get a notification when someone changes a note you shared. Revoke access at any time.

As a public page

Publish a note from your own private space as a clean article page, with a table of contents (‘On this page’), cover image, code highlighting and working live blocks. Share a whole folder and it gets its own page; new notes in it are public too.

Your public page lists what you've published. In the Share window, turn off ‘Show on my public page’ to keep a note off that list, or turn on ‘Hide from search engines’ to keep it out of search results.

Public notes are not encrypted, by design.

Live blocks: diagrams and tools that run inside the note

A code block can be more than code. Write Mermaid and get a diagram. Write SVG and get a drawing. Write HTML or React and get a working page: a calculator, a chart, a small dashboard. It runs inside your note, and on your public page too.

AI that runs on your computer

Journal's assistant runs a small language model in your browser. Nothing leaves your device.

The assistant works on desktop. Phones don't have enough memory for it. The default model is about 900 MB to download once.

  • Grammar checks with inline underlines.
  • Autocomplete as ghost text; press Tab to accept.
  • Rewrite in eight styles: Proofread, Rewrite, Friendly, Professional, Concise, Summary, Key Points, List/Table.
  • An assistant panel that can search and read your notes, and propose edits you accept or reject. /ask and /summarize open it.

Let your agents write in your notes, only where you allow

The Journal MCP server lets Claude Code, Claude Desktop and Codex CLI read, search, create and edit your notes. It has its own app registration on your identity, so you can revoke it separately.

Agents see nothing until you grant access. In Journal → Settings → Agent access, set each folder or note to none, read, or read and write. The default is none. Notes marked ‘exclude from AI’ are never readable.

FAQ

Do I need an account?

Yes, today you sign in with a Homebase identity, your own private space. You can try the demo first: john.doe.demo.rocks, password a.

What is Homebase?

An identity that belongs to you. Journal uses it for sync, sharing and sign-in. You don't need to know how it works to use Journal.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Notes live in a database in your browser. Changes sync when you're back online.

Are my notes encrypted?

The synced copy is encrypted. The copy on your device is not encrypted yet; passkey or passphrase unlock is in progress. Public notes are not encrypted, by design.

Can I move from Notion or Obsidian?

Yes. Import a Notion Markdown & CSV export or an Obsidian vault as a .zip in Settings → Data & storage. Links, images, tags and callouts come with it.

Can I take my notes out?

Yes. Export all notes as markdown in a ZIP with an assets folder.

Does AI see my notes?

The built-in assistant runs on your device. External agents through MCP see only the folders and notes you grant.

Is there a phone app?

Install Journal from your browser as an app on iPhone, Android, tablet or computer.

Will there be a paid plan?

Pro is planned for hosted extras. Details and prices aren't set yet.

Is it open source?

Yes, AGPL-3.0, on GitHub.

How do I use the key?

It comes by email with your receipt. Open Journal+ and enter the key to unlock the app on that device. Each device you enter it on counts toward the 5.

Do the apps need the internet?

Only now and then. The app checks the key when you enter it and about once a week after that. Without a connection it keeps working for 60 days.

What happens after a year?

The apps keep working, with every feature you had. Updates released after your year need a renewal: €14.99 adds another year to the same key, on this page.

Can I try the apps first?

Yes. Each app is a free download with a 3-day trial. Buy a key when the trial ends, or before.

Do I need a key for the web app?

No. Journal in the browser is free, with every feature it has, and you can install it from the browser as an app.

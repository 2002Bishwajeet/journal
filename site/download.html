Journal+ on your desktop

Journal+ is Journal as an app of its own for Mac, Windows and Linux. Sign in with your Homebase identity and your notes sync with the browser and your other devices.

  • Its own window, with its icon in the Dock or taskbar
  • Your notes stored on your computer, so it works offline
  • Updates itself, and asks before it restarts
  • Open source, like Journal in the browser

Desktop apps coming soon

Installing and updates

Each download is the latest release. Journal+ checks for a new one when it starts and once a day, downloads it in the background, and asks before it restarts.

Prefer to build it yourself? The app is open source: see the build notes on GitHub.

Mac
Open the .dmg and drag Journal+ to Applications. The app is signed and notarised by Apple.
Windows
Run the installer. It is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may say it protected your PC: choose More info, then Run anyway.
Linux
Make the AppImage executable and open it, or install the .deb with your package manager. The AppImage updates itself; for the .deb, install the new one from this page.
Your notes
Sign in with your Homebase identity. Notes you write in the app sync with the browser and your other devices.

Journal on your desktop

Journal runs as an app of its own on Mac, Windows and Linux. Sign in with your Homebase identity and your notes sync with the browser and your other devices.

  • Its own window, with its icon in the Dock or taskbar
  • Your notes stored on your computer, so it works offline
  • Updates itself, and asks before it restarts
  • Open source, like Journal in the browser

What the apps add

The apps have every feature of Journal in the browser, plus a few things only an app on your device can do.

  • Your notes are encrypted on your device too

    Your notes already sync encrypted. The app also encrypts the copy it keeps on your computer or phone.

    Mac, Windows, Linux, iPhone and iPad. On Linux without a system keychain, you set a passphrase instead. On Android, an experimental option in Settings.

  • Lock the app, or one note or folder

    Unlock with Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello or your fingerprint. Until you do, a locked note shows only its title.

    iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac and Windows.

  • Jot a note from anywhere

    Press Cmd+Shift+J on a Mac, or Ctrl+Shift+J on Windows and Linux, and a small window opens to type in, even when Journal is hidden. On a Mac, the menu bar icon opens it too.

    Mac, Windows and Linux. Not yet in a Linux Wayland session.

  • Stays running, starts at login, updates itself

    Close the window and Journal keeps syncing from the tray, or the menu bar on a Mac. It can start when you log in, and it installs new versions for you.

    Mac, Windows and Linux. The Linux .deb package does not update itself.

  • Share links open in the app

    On a phone, a link to a shared note opens in Journal. On a computer, the note's page has an Open in app button, and on a Mac, links you click in Mail or Messages open in Journal directly.

    iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac, Windows and Linux.

  • Writing help from Apple's on-device model

    Autocomplete, grammar and rewrite use the model built into your iPhone or iPad, so there is nothing to download.

    iPhone and iPad with Apple Intelligence.

Everything else is the same Journal you use in the browser, which stays free.

After you install

Journal signs in with a Homebase identity, a private space that belongs to you and holds your notes.

  1. Create a Homebase identity

    Create one at homebase.id, where sign-up is in closed beta.

  2. Sign in

    Open Journal and enter your identity. If you already use Journal in the browser, sign in with the same one and your notes are there.

Installing and updates

Each download is the latest release. The app checks for a new one when it starts and once a day, downloads it in the background, and asks before it restarts.

Prefer to build it yourself? The app is open source: see the build notes on GitHub.

Mac
Open the .dmg and drag Journal to Applications. The app is signed and notarised by Apple.
Windows
Run the installer. It is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may say it protected your PC: choose More info, then Run anyway.
Linux
Make the AppImage executable and open it, or install the .deb with your package manager. The AppImage updates itself; for the .deb, install the new one from this page.
Your notes
Sign in with your Homebase identity. Notes you write in the app sync with the browser and your other devices.

What Free covers

All of it, today. Your notes live on your device and in your own Homebase space.

  • Writing Markdown shortcuts, a slash menu, tables, task lists, callouts, math and code.
  • Live blocks Mermaid, SVG, HTML and React that run in the note.
  • Encrypted sync Across your devices, with the app working offline.
  • Sharing With other Homebase identities and circles, who edit with you.
  • Public pages Publish a note from your own space for anyone with the link.
  • Version history Restore an earlier version as one edit you can undo.
  • Import and export Notion and Obsidian in, markdown in a ZIP out.
  • Organising Folders, tags, note links, daily notes and templates.
  • Agents Claude Code, Claude Desktop and Codex CLI, in the folders you grant.
  • Light and dark Install it as an app on any device, in either theme.

Questions about paying

Do I pay anything today?

No. Every feature in Journal today is on the Free plan.

What does the Homebase identity cost?

Journal doesn't sell identities or charge for them. Homebase is invite-only during its alpha: join the wait list at homebase.id, which sets its own terms.

What will Pro be?

A paid plan for hosted extras. Its price and contents aren't decided yet; follow issue #306 to see them take shape.

Can I run Journal myself?

Yes. Journal is open source under the AGPL-3.0, on GitHub.

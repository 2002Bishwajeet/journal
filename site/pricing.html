What Free covers

All of it, today: everything that lives on your device and in your own Homebase space.

Pro will add hosted extras, planned in the open in issue #306.

Writing
Markdown shortcuts and a slash menu: tables, task lists, callouts, math and code.
Live blocks
Mermaid, SVG, HTML and React that run in the note.
Encrypted sync
Across your devices, with the app working offline.
Sharing and public pages
With other Homebase identities and circles, who edit with you, or as a public page for anyone with the link.
Version history
Restore an earlier version as one edit you can undo.
Agents
Claude Code, Claude Desktop and Codex CLI, in the folders you grant.
Import and export
Notion and Obsidian in, markdown in a ZIP out.

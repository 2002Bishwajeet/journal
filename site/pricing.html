Questions about paying

Do I have to pay to use Journal?

No. Journal in the browser is free, with every feature. Only the Journal+ apps are paid, after a 3-day trial.

How do I get a Homebase identity?

Join the Homebase wait list at homebase.id. Homebase sets its own terms.

What do I get when I buy Journal+?

A license key by email. Enter it in the app on up to 5 of your devices. It includes a year of updates from the day you pay, and the apps keep working after the year. Another year of updates costs €14.99. More about Journal+.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once, and nothing renews on its own. Renewing for more updates is a separate purchase you choose to make.

Can I run Journal myself?

Yes. Journal is open source under the AGPL-3.0, on GitHub.

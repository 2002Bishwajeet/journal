Questions about paying

Do I have to pay to use Journal?

No. Journal in the browser is free for writing, sync, sharing, agents, and import and export. Only the Journal apps are paid, after a 3-day trial. They add your notes encrypted on your device, a Face ID, Touch ID or Windows Hello lock, and quick capture from anywhere.

How do I get a Homebase identity?

Join the Homebase wait list at homebase.id. Homebase sets its own terms.

What do I get when I buy the apps?

The same Journal as in the browser, plus what only an app can do, like encrypting your notes on your device and locking them. You also get a year of updates from the day you pay, and the apps keep working after the year. On Mac, Windows and Linux you get a license key by email for up to 5 of your devices. Another year of updates costs €14.99. More about the apps.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once, and nothing renews on its own. Renewing for more updates is a separate purchase you choose to make.

Can I run Journal myself?

Yes. Journal is open source under the AGPL-3.0, on GitHub.

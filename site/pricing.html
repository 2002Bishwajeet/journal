Questions about paying

Do I pay anything today?

No. Every feature in Journal today is on the Free plan.

What does the Homebase identity cost?

Journal doesn't sell identities or charge for them. Homebase is invite-only during its alpha: join the wait list at homebase.id, which sets its own terms.

What will stay free?

What's stored on your own Homebase: notes, folders, sync, sharing with people, the local assistant, import and export.

Will public notes change?

The plan is that Free keeps up to three public notes when Pro arrives. Going back to Free never un-publishes a note that is already public.

Why would anything cost money?

Link previews and blogs run on servers the project pays for. Pro covers those, not the notes in your own space.

Can I run Journal myself?

Yes. Journal is open source under the AGPL-3.0, on GitHub.

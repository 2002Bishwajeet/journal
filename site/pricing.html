What Free covers

All of it, today: everything that lives on your device and in your own Homebase space.

Pro will add hosted extras. Which ones is still to be decided.

Writing
Markdown shortcuts and a slash menu: tables, task lists, callouts, math and code.
Live blocks
Mermaid, SVG, HTML and React that run in the note.
Encrypted sync
Across your devices, with the app working offline.
Sharing and public pages
With other Homebase identities and circles, who edit with you, or as a public page for anyone with the link.
Version history
Restore an earlier version as one edit you can undo.
Agents
Claude Code, Claude Desktop and Codex CLI, in the folders you grant.
Import and export
Notion and Obsidian in, markdown in a ZIP out.

Questions about paying

Do I pay anything today?

No. Every feature in Journal today is on the Free plan.

How do I get a Homebase identity?

Join the Homebase wait list at homebase.id. Homebase sets its own terms.

What will Pro be?

A paid plan for hosted extras. Its price and contents aren't decided yet; follow issue #306 to see them take shape.

Can I run Journal myself?

Yes. Journal is open source under the AGPL-3.0, on GitHub.

### Changed

- On a phone, the dim behind a sheet is lighter in light mode. It fades with the sheet, and it becomes lighter while you drag the sheet down.
- On a phone, rows in action sheets and pickers change color while you press them. Cancel is a separate outlined button.
- On a phone, the note header has the same height as the list headers. Its line and its small title show when you scroll the note.
- On a phone, when you zoom an image in the viewer, the buttons and the caption go away. They come back when you zoom out.

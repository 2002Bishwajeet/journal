### Fixed

- A note that someone shares with you opens from its link. Journal now asks for the "Receive notes from people" permission when you sign in. If you do not have it, the link shows the permission prompt, and the note opens after you grant it.

### Changed

- Journal does not ask all of your connections for shared notes every 10 minutes. It gets a shared note when its owner shares it with you, when you open its link, or when you tap Refresh in "Shared with me".
- When an owner stops sharing a note with you, the owner's Journal sends you a notification, and your Journal then removes the note from "Shared with me".

### Fixed

- The desktop app uses much less memory when you have many notes open in tabs. Only your current tab and the three you used most recently stay loaded. Other tabs reload when you switch to them, at the same scroll position.
- Charts and other live blocks you have scrolled well past unload, and load again when you scroll back to them.
- A note that changes on another device, or is edited by an agent such as Claude, now reloads once instead of twice.
